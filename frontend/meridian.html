The Process

From KYC to live
stablecoin in minutes.

01
Identity

Pass KYC

Connect your wallet, complete identity verification. Individuals and businesses both supported. Your jurisdiction, your regulations — we verify identity, not compliance.

02
Reserves

Prove Reserves

Link a Chainlink Proof of Reserve feed confirming your backing assets. USD fiat, T-bills, USDC, USDT — any approved asset. The protocol verifies. Minting is disabled until reserves are confirmed.

03
Deploy

Launch Your Token

Name it. Symbol it. Set your peg currency. The Meridian factory deploys a fully-audited CW20 stablecoin contract on Amitis Network. Your contract, your minters, your whitelist.

04
Mint & Burn

Operate

Mint tokens against verified reserves. Your users hold and transfer within your ecosystem. When they redeem — burn, and release the backing fiat. Protocol fees are collected automatically.

05
Liquidity

Connect to DeFi

Your stablecoin is IBC-enabled on day one. List on AmitisΔex, bridge to Osmosis, connect to any Cosmos chain. The entire Cosmos liquidity layer is one channel away.

06
Scale

Grow

Add minters, expand whitelists, update reserve URIs. Meridian retains an admin override for compliance purposes — but your stablecoin's operations are yours to run.

The Protocol Rules

Non-negotiable.
By design.

Meridian enforces a small set of protocol-level rules on every stablecoin issued through the factory. These cannot be waived. They cannot be upgraded away. They are the trust layer that makes every Meridian-issued token credible.

Everything else — who can mint, who can hold, what the token is called, how it circulates — is entirely up to the issuer.

⛓
Full Reserve Required Circulating supply must never exceed verified reserves. Chainlink Proof of Reserve enforces this on-chain.
✅
Approved Assets Only Backing assets must be from Meridian's approved list: USD fiat, T-bills, USDC, USDT. No algorithmic or undercollateralized backing.
🔍
KYC Before Deploy Every issuer is identity-verified before the factory will deploy their contract. Pseudonymous issuance is not supported.
💰
Protocol Fee on Mint/Burn 10 basis points on every mint and burn event. Collected automatically by the factory. Non-removable.
🛡
Meridian Override Meridian retains the ability to pause or freeze any issued contract for compliance purposes. An escape valve, not a control mechanism.

The Flywheel

Every mint and burn on any Meridian stablecoin is a transaction on Amitis Network. Those transactions trigger the 0.3% MonetaryFeeDecorator — 80% rebated to users, 20% to validators.

More Meridian issuers → more on-chain volume → more validator revenue → more valuable validator petals → stronger VPT collateral → stronger institutional position.

Every stablecoin issued here makes the network more valuable.
