Proof of Reserves

Configure your reserve attestation. Minting will be disabled until a valid Chainlink PoR feed is linked.

🔗

Chainlink Proof of Reserve is required. Your PoR feed must publish the total reserves backing your stablecoin. Circulating supply can never exceed verified reserves. Set up your feed at chain.link/proof-of-reserve before deploying.

Must return a JSON feed that Chainlink oracles can verify. Format: {"total_reserves": "1000000.00", "currency": "USD", "timestamp": 1234567890}
